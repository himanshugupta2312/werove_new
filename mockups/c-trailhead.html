FAQ

Good questions

Ask anything else on WhatsApp.

How much does a trip cost?

It depends on the park, the number of safaris, the stay and group size. You'll get an all-in price with your itinerary, before you pay anything.

Can you guarantee a tiger sighting?

No one honestly can. We maximise your chances with the right season, zones and number of drives, and our naturalists know the territories.

Do I need experience for birding trips?

Not at all. We pace walks for beginners and can lend binoculars. Serious birders can tell us their target species.

How far ahead should I book?

Safari permits for peak months (March–May) sell out early, so 60–90 days ahead is ideal. Birding trips are more flexible.
