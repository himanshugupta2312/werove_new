Our story

For the travellers, by the travellers

We Rove began on a Republic Day in Corbett, among a mountaineer-turned-naturalist, a bird lover and friends who'd had enough of corporate weekends.

We're still small. We plan every itinerary ourselves, know the naturalists by name, and go on most trips. No names here, just what each of us does.
