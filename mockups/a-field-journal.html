How we travel

The animal comes first.
The photo second.

Our one rule: leave life alone and undisturbed. In the field, that means a few things we don't bend on, even if it costs us a sighting.

  • No playback. Ever.We don't play bird calls to pull birds out. It stresses them, especially in breeding season.
  • Distance over closenessWe keep back from nests, roosts and animals, and we don't chase anything that moves off.
  • No baiting, no flashWe don't feed wildlife for photographs, and we don't use flash on birds or at night.
  • Small groups, local peopleSmall groups only. We work with local naturalists, drivers and homestays.
  • We carry it outEvery wrapper and bottle comes back with us.
  • Season-awareWe don't push into forests during closures or into sensitive areas off-route.
