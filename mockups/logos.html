Logo concepts

The current logo, a hiker on a mountain, reads as a trekking company. Every concept below says safari and birding. Each one is shown on light and dark backgrounds, next to the name, and at browser-tab size. These are concept sketches, not final artwork.

1 · Striped Feather

A bird's feather wearing tiger stripes: both halves of the business in one shape. It's simple enough to work as a favicon, an Instagram avatar, a jeep sticker or embroidery.

5 · Field Pass Stamp

Like an old forest-department entry pass. Full of character on printed itineraries, packaging and social posts. It works better as a secondary badge alongside concept 1 or 4 than as the main logo, because it's too wide for a favicon.

Secondary

6 · The Arch, evolved

Keeps the shape and colours of your current logo, the yellow arch over a horizon, so existing followers still recognise it. The hiker and snow peaks are swapped for forest, foothills and birds. This is the safe choice if you want continuity.
