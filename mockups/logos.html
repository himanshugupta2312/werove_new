Logo concepts

New: Your original logo, refined: the same sunrise dome, hiker and pines, redrawn more cleanly, with a version that adds the tiger and a one-colour print version.

The current logo, a hiker on a mountain, reads as a trekking company. Every concept below says safari and birding. Concepts 7–9 were added after the competitor study. Concepts 10–14 come from your own story and words: the Open Book, the Rover community, the founders, "adventure is out there", and the name itself. Each one is shown on light and dark backgrounds, next to the name, and at browser-tab size. These are concept sketches, not final artwork.

A bird's feather wearing tiger stripes: both halves of the business in one shape. It's simple enough to work as a favicon, an Instagram avatar, a jeep sticker or embroidery.

Recommendation: use 1 · Striped Feather (or, for an Indian boho direction, 7 · Stripe Arch) as the main mark, with the name set in Fraunces, and keep 5 · Field Pass as a secondary badge for itineraries and social posts. Once you pick a direction, have a designer refine it into final vector files (about a day's work). These sketches are meant for choosing a direction, not for print.

If you want a logo that is true to your story: my picks are 10 · Open Book (your own mission quote, and it reads instantly) and 13 · Compass Feather (keeps the feather you liked and gives it a job). 12 · Ice Axe & Feather is the origin story but busier at small sizes; 11 and 14 are the weakest visually.
