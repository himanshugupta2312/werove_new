# Destination page data (copy this once per place)

Each destination gets its own page (so people can find it on Google) plus its pin on the map. Fill in what you can; anything blank shows as "coming soon" or is left out. **Please have someone who has been there check the species and season facts.**

```
SLUG (used in the web address):   e.g. tadoba           -> werove.in/destinations/tadoba
NAME:                              Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve
STATE / REGION:                    Maharashtra
TYPE (tick):                       [x] Tiger   [x] Birding   [ ] Leopard   [ ] Cheetah   [ ] Rhino   [ ] Elephant
                                   (tiger reserves are also birding spots by default)

ONE-LINE SUMMARY:                  (appears on the map card and in search results)
ABOUT (2-3 short paragraphs):      what makes this place special, in your words
WHAT YOU MAY SEE (mammals):        e.g. tiger, leopard, sloth bear, dhole, gaur
BIRDS TO LOOK FOR:                 e.g. 4-6 species your birders are sure about
BEST MONTHS:                       e.g. March to May (and why)
OPEN / CLOSED:                     e.g. core zones close roughly July to September
HOW TO REACH:                      nearest airport / railway station, drive time
TYPICAL TRIP LENGTH:               e.g. 3 nights, 5 safaris
SAFARI DETAILS:                    zones we usually book, morning/evening, jeep or other
STAYS:                             type of stay you use (lodge / homestay / resort), no names needed
SAMPLE ITINERARY (day by day):     Day 1 ... Day 2 ...
INCLUDED (this place):             anything different from the general list
NOT INCLUDED (this place):
STARTING PRICE (optional):         e.g. "from Rs ... per person" or "on request"
GOOD TO KNOW:                      permits, ID, age rules, what to carry
MAP POSITION:                      I'll place it; tell me if it looks wrong
PHOTOS:                            put 3-5 in  destinations/<slug>/  (see FOLDER-GUIDE)
```

**If a destination has no photos yet,** its page and map card show a **"Photos coming soon"** picture, so you can launch without them.
